=== VERDICT: NOT A DEFECT — A DOCUMENTATION GAP, and it is on the box's side ===
The runner OWNS the byte freeze during a batch: _run_one_gate_core() hashes the frozen paths
before and after the child and writes freeze-before.json, freeze-after.json and freeze-verdict.txt
into the run dir, and ANY drift halts the whole runner. The child neither can nor should take that
measurement itself — that is precisely why the hook denies it. But nothing tells the child so, and
rails §2 reads as an unconditional duty on "a session".
