# date: 2026-08-29T13:45:01Z

THE DEFECT (GATE-RUNNER-ARM, 2026-08-28). `--reset-breaker` wrote a HARDCODED
  "reset_by": "operator via --reset-breaker"
into breaker.json. It was written when the BOX ran the reset, not the operator — so the single
record of who cleared a safety breaker said something false, permanently, in the file an operator
would later read to find out exactly that.

THE FIX. Record what is OBSERVABLE and label its limits. uid cannot discriminate the operator from
the box on this host (both are kalib, NOPASSWD: ALL) — so nothing here claims WHO. The one honest
discriminator available is whether stdin was a terminal, and it is labelled as evidence, not proof.
