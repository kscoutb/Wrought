# date: 2026-08-28T18:50:00Z

## WHY THIS IS NOT COSMETIC — the hole HARDEN's isolation left open.
## Measured at the start of this gate (raw/08, the build_child_env dump): a gate child was handed
##     DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS=unix:path=/run/user/1000/bus
## i.e. THE REAL SESSION BUS, even though HARDEN gives it a private $HOME and a private
## $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR. The steering fix moved the cc-socks surface and left the bus surface
## untouched. The session bus is a cross-session vector; a child that holds its address can
## reach services on it regardless of what its runtime dir says.

## ---- STEP 1: does the RUNNER PARENT need it? (deciding total drop vs child-only strip) ----

##        systemd-run --user --scope --quiet --unit=arm-dbustest-$$.scope -p RuntimeMaxSec=30 \
##        /bin/echo "SCOPE-RAN-WITHOUT-DBUS"
  SCOPE-RAN-WITHOUT-DBUS
  rc=0        <<< the launcher does NOT need it

## cmd (CONTROL): the same with XDG_RUNTIME_DIR ALSO removed
  Failed to connect to user scope bus via local transport: $DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS and
  $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR not defined (consider using --machine=<user>@.host --user ...)
  rc=1        <<< and this is the exact failure HARDEN's probe C1 hit

## => sd-bus DERIVES the bus socket path from XDG_RUNTIME_DIR. Because the parent does not need
##    the variable, the drop is TOTAL (removed from the `keep` allowlist outright) rather than a
##    child-only strip via the argv `env` prefix. That is the tighter of the two surfaces the
##    prompt allowed, and it needs no conditional logic that could silently stop applying.
