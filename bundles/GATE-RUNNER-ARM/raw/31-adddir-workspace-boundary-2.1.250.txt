=== VERDICT: PASS — UNCHANGED FROM 2.1.238 ===
Under `dontAsk`, a Bash command whose target lies OUTSIDE the session's working directory is
DENIED **even when --allowedTools explicitly permits that command**, and naming the directory with
--add-dir is what lifts the denial. The workspace boundary is a real, independent containment
layer on 2.1.250, and it is the reason /etc/wrought/runner.conf must carry `add_dirs` at all —
without it a gate cannot write its own bundle into the courier.

CORROBORATION FROM A REAL GATE, not just this probe: in Phase 5(ii) the gate child's cwd was
<S>/gatecwd while its bundle had to be written to <S>/courier — a different tree entirely — and it
succeeded precisely because the derived config's `add_dirs` named it (raw/25). The positive half of
this boundary is therefore proven twice, once in isolation and once in production shape.
