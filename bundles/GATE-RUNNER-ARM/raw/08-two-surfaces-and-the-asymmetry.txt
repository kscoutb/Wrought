## ===== THE LOAD-BEARING CONSEQUENCE =====
## HARDEN's ephemeral HOME — the fix for the cross-session STEERING breaker — RE-OPENED the
## autoupdate exposure for gate children, because it moves the child away from the very file
## the interactive fix lives in. Three independent reasons the settings block cannot reach a
## gate child: (1) HOME is the ephemeral dir, so ~/.claude/settings.json is not there; (2) the
## launch passes --setting-sources '' ; (3) the child env is an ALLOWLIST — nothing is inherited.
## => BOTH places are required. Neither is redundant. Fixing only one leaves a live hole,
##    and the child hole is the one that matters for an unattended batch.
