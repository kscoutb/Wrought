TWO SEPARATE DEFECTS, BOTH IN THE CHECK AND NEITHER IN THE RESULT:
  1. `pgrep -x qemu-system-x86_64` printed its own refusal --
     'pattern that searches for process name longer than 15 characters will result in zero
     matches'. `comm` is capped at 15 bytes by the kernel. A 'none' from that call is not a
     measurement; it is the tool declining to look.

     shebang is /opt/wrought/venv-orch/bin/python, so the process's `comm` is 'python3' --
     the name never appears where -x looks. It would have returned 'none' with the process
     running.

THE CORRECT METHOD is the one wrought-runner's reaper already uses and rails §13 records:
