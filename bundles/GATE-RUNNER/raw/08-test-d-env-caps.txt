=== FINDINGS (d) ===
1. CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS TAKES EFFECT, and fails LOUDLY and machine-readably: rc=1,
   is_error=true, terminal_reason='api_error', and a result string that names the variable.
   Note output_tokens read 256 against a cap of 64 — the accounting is not the cap; treat the
   number as unreliable and the ERROR as the signal.
2. BASH_DEFAULT_TIMEOUT_MS TAKES EFFECT (4s observed vs the 120000 ms default), BUT IT DOES NOT
   KILL ANYTHING. It *backgrounds* the overrunning command, which keeps running with output
   redirected to a task file. As a containment control it bounds only how long the model WAITS.
   => a runaway child process can only be stopped by the kernel layer (the systemd scope's
      RuntimeMaxSec, which kills the whole cgroup). This is precisely why containment cannot be
      left at the harness level, independent of the hooks question.
3. --max-budget-usd TAKES EFFECT and is the cleanest failure signature of the three:
   subtype='error_max_budget_usd', terminal_reason='budget_exhausted', rc=1.
   IT IS A SOFT CEILING: the observed spend OVERSHOT the cap by 4.6x, because the check happens
   between turns and a turn already in flight is paid for. Any STOP-4 accounting must therefore
   set the per-gate cap well BELOW the amount it can afford to lose, and must reconcile against
   total_cost_usd after the fact rather than trusting the cap to hold the line.
4. CROSS-CUTTING, and the single most important classification fact from Phase 1:
   `subtype` was 'success' even on the d1 hard failure. `is_error` and `terminal_reason` are the
   reliable discriminators; `subtype` is not, and the exit integer alone is not (test (b)).
