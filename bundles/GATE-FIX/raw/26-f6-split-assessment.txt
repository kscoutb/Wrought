## Half 1 (network isolation) needs the child's LAUNCH SHAPE changed, not a flag added.
$ grep -n "systemd-run\", \"--user\", \"--scope\"" bin/wrought-runner
970:        "systemd-run", "--user", "--scope", "--quiet", f"--unit={unit}",

   A systemd SCOPE cannot take the sandboxing properties at all:
$ systemd-run --user --scope --quiet -p PrivateNetwork=yes /bin/true
   exit=1  ->  Unknown assignment: PrivateNetwork=yes
$ systemd-run --user --quiet --wait -p PrivateNetwork=yes /bin/true   # a SERVICE accepts it
   exit=0  ->  (no output; accepted)

   So closing half 1 means moving the gate child from --scope to a service (or wrapping it
   in its own netns) AND giving it a deliberate path back to 127.0.0.1:8080. That changes
   process supervision, output capture, the deadman attach, and the reaper's scope-membership
   logic -- i.e. the whole child-launch architecture. It is the process-starting gate's work.
